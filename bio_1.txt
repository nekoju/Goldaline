We are entirely the result of someone taking the "country is just farm emo" meme too seriously.
The compellingly misshapen tonal fruit borne of the colliding loins of artistic absurdity and judicious self-effacement, Cattle Cult materialized as an experiment of unlikely juxtapositions.

So bring us your yearning for walls of drippingly reverberant feedback and for tenuous social metaphors and for sincere-but-simultaneously-too-literal-and-incomprehensibly-vague songwriting!
Bring us the joy reflected off your black mirrors when your eye makeup perfectly radiates your indwelt ennui like the sixty-cycle hum of a cheap Telecaster!
Bring us your affectation for tropes and cliches about trucks and your romance for varyingly lighthearted narratives about crippling addiction and the uniqueness of the American tragedy!
Bring us all your disdain for Capitalism and your predilection for petty vandalism and bureaucratically inconvenient subversion!
Bring us your shrillest of whistles and most guttaral of hollers!

Bring us your worship of pungent riffs and romantic self-loathing and we promise to give you the world.
