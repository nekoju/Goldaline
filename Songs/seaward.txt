Seaward

Kindly disregard the words I said inside you
Your body or your soul the choice is yours
Kindly make a note; forget the fucking music that we made
over drinking, over laughter, on all fours

Turn youself away and face the sea
Sail on past the dragons on the right
Or on the sea as you would call it 'starboard'
Like ships exchanging cannonfire in the night

Good faith toward the sirens hasn't really served me well
So faithless now I stand before good will
I've been far kinder than I'd like with all my words
But now I'll find the phrase to point you off and sail
You seaward
