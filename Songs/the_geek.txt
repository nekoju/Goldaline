The Geek


Two for a nickel, gather round and please silence your phones
In waiting for the creature who's living in the blackened below
A-sleepin' in straw for the odd opportunity to put on a show for you generous folks
And waiting for the people above it to throw it a bone

A tremoring reject of the twinkling Kingdom of God
Or an earnest, if comprmised, serial entripeneur
Beset by repeatedly finding its folly instead of the joy that it hoped
At the end of the innocent twist of a stylish rope

But enough of the gratuity of tired exposition
What you came for's just fixing to shriek and emerge
Given a body in a pit and and a feathery bundle of innocence
You'll happily forget what put it down in the dirt
