Teeth


With all the sugar that you push between your teeth each time you smile
It's a wonder that they're still as nice as yours
And with each saccharine kindsness you deliver darlin'
It's a wonder that you can't outshine the stars
But I know the meat that's lurking there beneath those pearly whites
Ain't as freshened as you'd like us to believe
And you laugh like a hyena at the pieces of folks you'd like to be

With every artisan perfume delivered to your door you think you'll make
Yourself the harder thus to see
Behind the cloud that trails you every morning from the bath
