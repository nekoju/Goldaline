Maybe we met somewhere else long ago
And wanted the same from the Earth
And maybe our spindly forms were entwined
Somewhere else long ago in the dirt
But here we stand naked and shining, below
The shimmering gate of the stars
Both wanting what others have failed to deliver
And both coming up empty of heart

Maybe we'll meet in the blackened beyond
With our spirits as white as the snow
And maybe we'll writhe in a sloppy embrace
And we'll laugh once again as we glow
Or maybe we won't and we'll just turn to dust
And our ancestors won't ever know
And after all that happens maybe we'll write
What we wanted the other to know
