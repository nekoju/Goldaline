Heat Upon Heat


Heat upon heat
The fragrant leavings of you mother's trite obsession
Heat upon heat
The boiling fatigue of tired self-reflection
Heat upon heat
The pain of wondering where you'll be and how you fit
Heat upon heat upon heat
Your very own shiny needle at your neck

Heat upon heat
The box you had no choice but grin and bear it
Heat upon heat
Another lecture from some cosmic poser sheriff
Heat upon heat
The ripping, searing, dripping of the wax that makes the flesh upon your bones
Heat upon heat upon heat upon heat upon heat
keep your chin upturned to waking up again all tired and alone

Heat upon heat
Another half-convincing "getting by"
Heat upon heat
Another half-deluded noble try
Heat upon heat
While lathering your face up one more time
And heat upon heat upon heat upon heat
To stay your ever-drooping eyes
