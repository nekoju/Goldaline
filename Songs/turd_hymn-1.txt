Turd Hymn: I


Oh, to be lectured on the goodness of living
By illiterates with semicolon tattoos
Oh, to be deemed an irrationalist
By a sentient, warm Mountain Dew
Oh, to be breathing, oh to be reading
The same air; differing books about God
And oh to be granted the gift of compassion
By a father who worships the rod

Oh, to feel doubt at the sacredest thing
You have learned or you ever will hold
Oh, upon finding its sacredness naught 
But the shape of the void in your skull
Oh, to be dancing, oh to be singing
The praises of Earth neath your feet
While the powers that be, all enamored of weeds
Coming in past the grasses
that prickle our asses 
and bloody our innocent soles

From a life spent a-taking the punches ahead of the worms
Oh to be ready to die and go down in the dirt
Oh to be radioactive because you've been hurt
