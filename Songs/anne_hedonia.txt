Anne Hedonia


Changed my middle name to something else to match the spirit of the first
And hoped that people hearing it would ask from whence it came before just going off to church
But instead they just kept pushing all the things they've all been hanging on since birth
But I guess it makes me feel better about myself for what it's bloody worth

"C'mon down to pride," they said while wearing all their rainbow Sunday best
So against my better judgement, got into my car and to the square I went
And everyone was happy, cheering, being the bestest versions of their beings
But poor on cash for tips, so I stay quietly behind  and thought of better things
