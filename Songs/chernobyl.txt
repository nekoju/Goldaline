My Own Chernobyl


My very own Chernobyl
And the worms around me glowin' in the ground
My own Ailanthus
Lapping up the salts that now surround
the particles of sand and lead and humus
Keeping clear the way of all that were around
My own Chernobyl

A tired metaphor
For human hubris but it fit so here we be
And I'm reciting
A tenuously thought-out half-deluded sobriquet
About "Is God homonculously pulling strings to steer my head
through every layer'f every shitty thing I said?"
--My own Chernobyl
