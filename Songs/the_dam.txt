The Dam

They built their little boxes in the valley
Unconcerned for all the water up above
Cause they built a wall of marble just above them
That leaked to keep them fed just well enough

Well the townsfolk, they just kept on havin' babies
Kept on building, kept on making what they wanted to see 
Built a hospital, a museum, and couple restaurants too
But the liquid left them short of what they need

So they met in coalition on an August afternoon
To figure what they needed now to do
And planned to mitigate the the risk to make that hole a little bigger
And let a bit more hopeful liquid through

They had art and hospitality; they really did their best
With the energy the trickling deluge brought
But their basements all got wet
So choked the over-trickling water off

But the force had grown accustomed
To the resistance of its hole, as present in the cement-clad facade
So it pushed upon the cracks that had been left around the hole
Where stone integrity collides with what we want

And so it crashed to bottom; the people in their homes
Drowned pressed against the walls
Because they made a home in chaos held
By little more than spit and sloth
