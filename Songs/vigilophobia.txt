vigilophobia


I tried to find the word for when your dreaming and your waking lie there intertwined as one
I tried to find the others surely paranoid of ever waking up
I tried thinking on some reasons I should ever want to be what I've become
But I couldn't so I just turned all of the lights out, left just well enough alone

The letters on the menu took the shapes of propaganda from another place and time
And a smile, far more legible, met my needing with a valid reason why
I should "go on heaving, rushing, breathing onward, ever-by"
And some noise or another from my phone set me to gasping in the light

And so the morning came; I set to doing what I should
Set to checking off the boxes with a soul made out of wood
Set to reading papers set with letters that I knew and understood
Mourning what I couldn't; forgetting everything that ever was or ever could
