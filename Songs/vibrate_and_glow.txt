Vibrate and Glow

And I saw it in the distance
A million foreheads pressed together
Purple vines that reach from eye to eye!

And I saw it in the distance
The farmers with their hearts together
Stamping down the kings beneath their tines

But then I saw it coming closer
Saw the eyeballs of its raging gaining
Coughing something out along the lines of "seven, seven times!"

Saw my comrades waving up ahead
Like Ewoks singing all together
"Crush the fucker's head with logs and vines!"

And we'll all begin to vibrate
And behold we'll all begin to glow
And we'll float right on up the the heavens above
A great golden ring for the great golden vibratin' globe
