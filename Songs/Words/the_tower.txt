The Tower


The Artist said "Let's build a tower.
"eighty cubits high
And ten around, basaltic walls,
That reaches to the sky!"

So the builders set to building
Our enforcers gathered up
The oligarchs and sycophants 
That murdered what we love

Then there was some semblance of a trial
But everyone around already saw
What anyone with power did to anyone they could
And calmly turned those tender feelings off

Dispatched so procedurally
With naught a grin or gloat
They dragged their bodies up the walls
Of the stony telescope

Again resigned and not a laugh
they threw the bodies in
A layer cake of flesh and lime
A trauma-blackened tin

They filled it up with water
And they lowered the pH
And left the cake alone a century
for the rot of it to bake

And once the day had finally come
To crack and slough the walls
It stood there gleaming in the sun
as black as the basalt

A thousand putrid figures
In lifeless agonizing naught
Reflecting of the glint and heat
of history's judgement wraught

And there is stood millenia
While bureaucrats walked by
To get to offices humbled now
beneath the sallow sky

In case the bricks that made it
Ever deigned to compromise
The boundaries of the structure
Once inhabited by flies

And so it stands this very day
A stack of human sin
To remind us all of what we've done
And never die again
