A knife between friends, pt 2

"What's a knife between friends", I asked rhetorically
(Though honestly, half-waiting for an answer)
While (Wolf am I!) I licked the frosty, sanguine blade outside your door
I might as well rhetorically ask you, "buddy, why'd you put it there?"
But the answer might as well be just the same as that before

While my glass upturned, half-full each of milk and bile on the counter
Abandoned to the morning's cold routine without a towel
Cursing at a razor made for stubble, turned to task on baby trees
Now the bastard in the mirror as I'm shaving says, "Dear lover,"
"Why'd you fold to wrath and turn like crumbly, brittle Autumn leaves?"

Let's take a quick vacation
Remind us that we've come so far
Let's step back a moment
And make the windows new, unbarred
Let's build a brand new killing tower
Unburdened with the meat and bread
of fleshy expectation
Set into place the rocks that make us who we are

God and Justice had no further answers than a year ago
Though this time when I asked, the pleading bent to inquisition
While (Shadow am I!) I asked again, this time as wryly as intended
The answer came to fore without expecting
"Lover, comrade, sheave of grain; we're always only jesting"
