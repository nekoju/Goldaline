Homestead Centralia

I83 in the backseat of a shitty, red Corolla
Grains of sand dug firmly in my ass
Half-hearing Elise's diatribes about the apostates and queers
Half asleep, avoiding all of that

Spent the weekend walking 'cross the sand in Delaware
Picking up dead jellyfish and throwing them toward the sun
Sadly, though that's all I can remember
That approximates what anyone calls good

And then much later traveled North to see the mines
Where greed and folly kept on burning underneath
And made the limestone glow send that toxic light into the sky
for one surreally dim illuminated week

The cliffs glowed red like graphite at Chernobyl
Or bone inside a kiln to bring it home
Dad could've built his house on flesh if he were wiser
Not another 18 years of burning stone

Couldn't lift the bottle; couldn't even lift his arms
So he drank his three Sam Adams with a straw
A day away from me and dead
Crumpled, soiled, lonely frame upon the floor

Death is coming next if you're a lucky one
If you draw The Lovers, be afraid
Eyes like burning anthracite that beckon
Cast out the misery you know and meet the change
