The Dreamtime

It's AD twenty-twenty-five and everyone who's ever suffer'd's screaming
I can hear it in the echoes on the news
I can see their sallow faces peeking out between the cracks
Of the buildings where the men in suits make rules

It's AD thirty-four and things are looking grimmer than they've looked in quite a while
I see my neighbor and the one who taught my youngest one her math
Packed in trailers made for horses getting pulled by ugly trucks across the heartland
Because the rails in good 'ol 'merica sorely lack
