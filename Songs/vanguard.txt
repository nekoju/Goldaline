Vanguard


Take your bejewled, weightless cudgel and bring it atop of my head
Tell all your middlin' followers of speaking of the one who will rightly have fled
Wrongly or rightly, the one with a spritely little spring in her delicate tread
Will be mashed right on down, brought to horrible ground on the eve of his horrible death

History lofts weight of the assumptions of winnin' o'er the blaze of the plains of the homeland and heart
And the aroma of comfort suits many who've never believed in the creatures who whisper our art
And if you wonder a moment just leaving your being just prejudice-waving alone at the manager's door 
You'll find we're all mad but no madder than those who'll constrain a young river to start

Believing what you should and surely must to keep the fires burning bright
Dismissing what you're made of, checking boxes, keeping well there in the fight
And taking nothing but your meaning from the words of poets, drunks, and thieves
And voting for the progress of us all, but darlin', most of all, your being
