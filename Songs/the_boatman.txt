I went and bought slushie with the tokens that they put upon my eyes
And stumbled back to pick up on the weathered trail before I turned away
The boatman's hand extended for the fare I quietly shrugged and then replied
"Take the half remaining" and I handed him the last of what remained

A saw a starving orphan, sitting, begging for some scraps upon the bank
Beset afore the shining hills, just playing in the dirt among the reeds
And God appeared before me with a look like he's expecting what I think
So I thanked him for the glory of the contrast, turning walked along the beach

The river of the dead a-quietly churning there beneath my being, fledged
All filled with eels and name-defying creatures I can't fathom there beneath
All champ and gnash their teeth a-tippy-tappin' at the lonely, steely keel
Of the stoic little dinghy sailing, carrying only what remains of me
