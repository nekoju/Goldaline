\begin{verse}
\poemtitle{A knife between friends}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\tiny
You came down and out \\
Not knowing where you'd be or what you'd be without \\
Being full of pity's quite a way to live \\
So of course I told you off the cuff to 'come on in'

Up front, I'm a Jew \\
Just the funny kind your mom would like, not the kind who runs the news \\
So I was maybe just a little scared \\
When you started shouting, raving that I'd better be prepared

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}\\
\hspace{2em} What's a knife between friends? \\
\hspace{2em} What's a kindness to be done and what's a trouble to preempt \\
\hspace{2em} What's a person; who's a problem \\
\hspace{2em} And why does the means deal the same stack of cards as the ends?

I feel it twisting in my chest \\
Kind of like a knife, I guess, but doubtful it compares \\
Moral angst hits differently, beloved \\
When the bastard in the mirror is a shill who isn't buying her own wares

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}

\emph{What am I doing and why did I come}, I asked two enforcers of order, undone \\
To the magistrate, \emph{What's this thing, `affidavit'?} \\
\textcolor{Red}{``Just irregular, apathetic, atomic vibration''} \\
\emph{What's a libelous roommate; what's a murderous spouse?} \\
The clerk replied, \textcolor{Red}{``they're just the same except whose name is on the house''} \\
Again before Heaven, \emph{Why did I come? What makes us blush and what makes us feel numb?} \\
\emph{What's hope? What's desolation? What makes someone a mother?} \\
\textcolor{Red}{``Just bags full of chemicals bumping into each other.''}

$\hfill\blacksquare$

\end{multicols}
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
\poemtitle{Who the fuck knows?}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\tiny
Does it cause you cognitive dissonance\\
When it turns out that the one who said they loved you really didn't?\\
And does seeing people happy make you angry\\
Cause your parents fought that Saturday and ruined your fucking picnic?\\
Does it make it any easier to swallow\\
Your pointless fucking fate to know it's fucking pointless anyway\\
And will your lawyer even show?\\
\textcolor{Red}{Who the fuck knows?} [See eq. \ref{eq:lk-instablity}]

Would I be doing any differently\\
If someone hadn't told me time again I'm just emotionally indulgent?\\
Do I owe them for the privilege of fulfilling\\
What I'd never do without another just and proper scolding?\\
Is there really any telling\\
What else I would be saying and the rates I would be paying\\
for the Camaro and the nanny and the clothes?\\
\textcolor{Red}{Who the fuck knows?}

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}\\
\hspace{2em} Well we're all in this infernal thing together \\
\hspace{2em} And every parallel dimension's probably fucked \\
\hspace{2em} So instead of frettin' on my scars I'm gonna fret on my guitar \\
\hspace{2em} Gonna drink a beer and write a song and give a fuck

Does it cause me cognitive dissonance \\
To be so proudly humanistic but so petty when I'm writing? \\
Yes it does; I do it anyway \\
Cause I don't sleep a lot the nights I'm down and I don't feel like fighting \\
On the other hand it's quite auspicious \\
Cause I'd otherwise be living as a drone who's ever giving \\
Of its body and its mind and of its soul \\
\textcolor{Red}{But who the fuck knows?}

\textcolor{Red}{
\hspace{2em} Well the universe ain't got a lot of answers \\
\hspace{2em} But it's got a lot of questions you can ask \\
\hspace{2em} That are better for your mind than what the light of what's behind \\
\hspace{2em} Has got to show you 'bout the shadows of your past
}

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}\\
\hspace{2em} ... \\
\hspace{2em} And instead of lilting backlit shadows cast by all my rotten luck \\
\hspace{2em} Gonna drink a beer with someone else who gives a fuck

\\

\end{array}
\end{equation}

$\hfill\blacksquare$

\end{multicols}
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
\poemtitle{Superfund}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\tiny
What kind of shit you have to step over to get to me? \\
Did you miss the tower of cans and stack of dirty magazines? \\
Let me show you how I do it left to mine, my dear \\
And I'll get you home by ten if you don't wanna stay here

Check with the ranger at the gate \\
Get your name badge on your breast affixed and meet me at the lake \\
We call it that, but really it's a pond \\
But it's full of life you'll never find by looking at it wrong

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}\\
\hspace{2em} Cause I'm a barrel full of mercury darling \\
\hspace{2em} I'm an overflowing shitty lagoon \\
\hspace{2em} I'm a river full of hydrogen sulfide \\
\hspace{2em} I'm a picture of a smog-tinted moon \\
\hspace{2em} And I'm a burning stack of tires at twilight \\
\hspace{2em} An impenetrable chlorine haze \\
\hspace{2em} I'm a barrel of ammonium nitrate \\
\hspace{2em} I'm a lazy bureaucratic delay

What kind of nature do you expect to see? \\
You'll miss it if you look for something boring, lush, and green \\
I can take you to the fertilizer spill \\
Where I take my lunches in the shade of the wrongly colored hill \\
The trees grow weird and crooked by the lake \\
With the animals deformed and their uncountable legs \\
Do you see the puddle with its iridescent sheen? \\
Do you mind its iridescence beyond iridescency?

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS} \\
\hspace{2em} ... \\
\hspace{2em} And there are tadpoles in the languishing craters \\
\hspace{2em} Maybe the occasional frog \\
\hspace{2em} Just know that I'm a Superfund, baby \\
\hspace{2em} Remediate my landscape all night long

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}\\
\hspace{2em} ... \\
\hspace{2em} And if you wanna find some beauty, you'll find it \\
\hspace{2em} Succession is a valuable tool \\
\hspace{2em} But any patch of laurel that you find \\
\hspace{2em} Was rooted in the slurry before you

\hspace{2em} And there are tadpoles in the languishing craters \\
\hspace{2em} Maybe the occasional frog \\
\hspace{2em} Told ya I'm a Superfund, baby \\
\hspace{2em} I'll remediate my landscape when you're gone

$\hfill\blacksquare$

\end{multicols}
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
\poemtitle{Deadloop}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\tiny
Always gonna be lonely \\
Always gonna be sad \\
Always gonna be thinking 'bout the things that I don't have \\
Always gonna be clumsy \\
Always gonna be dumb \\
Always gonna write songs too overwrought to mean a fuck \\
Always gonna be sorry \\
Always gonna be wrong \\
Always gonna wonder where I'll end up when I screw the fucking dog

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS} \\
\hspace{2em} Always gonna say I'm never gonna be as clever as my dad wanted me to be \\
\hspace{2em} But after all, I'm doing better than the dice's snake eyes see \\
\hspace{2em} So whatever quantum bullshit is or isn't happening in my brain \\
\hspace{2em} Fuck the expectations, and oh yeah, fuck the state

Fuck being lonely \\
Fuck being sad \\
Fuck always thinking 'bout the things that I don't have \\
Fuck being clumsy \\
Fuck being dumb and \\
Fuck always feeling like the words I write are dealing empty hands \\
Fuck being sorry \\
Fuck being wrong and \\
Fuck all the condescending bastards that'll never understand

\hspace{2em} Cause I'm a multitude-containing, absent-minded fucking dunce \\
\hspace{2em} And some of all those vectors sure enough point elsewhere than but up \\
\hspace{2em} But wherever this bag of cold-cuts takes me that controls my fate \\
\hspace{2em} Fuck the expectations, and oh yeah, fuck the state

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS} [and while we're at it fuck the state]

$\hfill\blacksquare$

\end{multicols}
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
\poemtitle{You wrote 'em}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\tiny
I'm struggling to turn words into any kind of truth I can find anything to hang on \\
To make thunderous nonsense show all of the parts that I hate \\
And I got no intention of learning anything I can bank on \\
But I'll be standing up here singing to anyone listening 'til late

Mother of loves lost and banished, move me to sing an unspeakable truth someone'll heed \\
Send cleverly planted ideas that'll grow like a seed \\
If any among you can find any substance in any of the words that I say \\
You wrote 'em

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}
\hspace{2em} You wrote 'em; didn't put 'em to paper but you might just as well have held the pen \\
\hspace{2em} You wrote 'em; didn't come out of your lungs but your vacuum, it left me the wind \\
\hspace{2em} You wrote 'em; you didn't bloody your fingers to save an unsaveable rhyme \\
\hspace{2em} You wrote 'em; and you certainly didn't arrange 'em to match with the time
\hspace{2em} But you wrote 'em

I'm alone here again, with the company of the cross I so eagerly carry to bleed on \\
And I'm pacing the boulevard with pitiable paperboard signs \\
And even if you can't read 'em from traffic and even if you just keep keepin' on \\
You wrote em

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS}

And if you're looking for meaning in the cobbled-together, sanctimonious shit that I write \\
Let me tell you right now that I ain't got a clue \\
Cause you wrote 'em

\hspace{2em} \emph{CHORUS} [sure as fuck didn't arrange 'em yourself to match with the time]

$\hfill\blacksquare$

\end{multicols}
\end{verse}
